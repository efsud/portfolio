02 / ABOUT ME

A transportation engineer
who works with data.

I am a transportation modeller and data scientist specializing in transportation network analysis, optimization, machine learning, and data-driven mobility systems. I am currently completing my Ph.D. in Civil/Transportation Engineering at York University in Toronto.

My recent work includes parking pricing with the Toronto Parking Authority, parking and mode-choice optimization for North York General Hospital / Point A, and traffic analytics using roadside LiDAR with Ouster.

For my doctoral research, I develop predictive and optimization models for parking occupancy, demand, pricing, and network performance. I use graph-based models and transaction data to study how pricing can improve occupancy balance between neighbouring parking zones.

I’m interested in transit planning, travel-demand modelling, network optimization, and intelligent transportation systems—especially problems where transportation modelling, operations research, and geospatial analytics can help answer a practical question.
